Sources & historical note

Date note: the statute generally meant by “Indian Councils Act” is the Indian Councils Act 1892 (55 & 56 Vict. c. 14), which received royal assent on 20 June 1892. The Act enlarged legislative councils and allowed limited budget discussion and questions, but did not establish direct elections or give members control of the executive. The nomination arrangements are often described as an early, indirect form of representation. For further reading: the text of the Indian Councils Act 1892; B. Shiva Rao, the Framing of India’s Constitution: Select Documents, vol. 1; and the Encyclopaedia Britannica entry on the Indian Councils Acts.
